How a convoy runs

First time? This is the whole process.

  1. Check the scheduleThe game, map and start time are on this page and in the WhatsApp group.
  2. Get in the lobbyJoin the convoy server 15 minutes early. The host shares the meeting point.
  3. Line upPark in the order the host gives. Lights on, voice chat on.
  4. Drive the routeKeep your gap, obey the limit and call out if you need to drop.
  5. Park up and sharePost your screenshots on the Facebook page after we finish.

Convoy rules

Simple rules so everyone enjoys the drive.

Respect the limit

Stay at the convoy speed and keep a safe gap to the truck ahead.

No ramming or blocking

Accidents happen. Deliberate crashes and road blocking get you removed.

Listen to the host

The convoy lead sets the order, pace and rest stops.

Drop out politely

Need to leave? Say so in chat, move to the shoulder and rejoin when you can.

Be kind

Every VTC and independent driver is welcome. Keep chat friendly and free of abuse.

Streaming a GTC convoy

A few simple guidelines so viewers, hosts and drivers all have a good time.

Tell the host

Let the convoy host know you're going live before the convoy starts.

Name the convoy

Put GTC and the map in your stream title so viewers can find you.

Follow the rules on camera

The convoy rules apply to streamers too, including how you talk in chat and voice.
